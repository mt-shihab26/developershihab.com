# PaystubHero

## Overview

PaystubHero is a SaaS document generator for business owners, freelancers, independent contractors, and employees paid in cash. Users fill in a short form, pick a template, and download a ready-to-use PDF for proof of income, loan and rental applications, tax filing, or payroll records. The site reports more than 839,000 customers served.

## How It Works

**Choose a Template** – Pick one of 10 paystub designs, or choose another document type.

**Enter Details** – Add company, employee, work schedule, and salary details. Taxes, deductions, and year-to-date totals are calculated automatically.

**Download** – Pay through a secure checkout, then download the PDF instantly or get it by email. Past documents stay in the user's profile for re-download.

## Documents

- **Paystubs** – US and Canadian generators with 10 templates
- **Tax Forms** – W-2, W-9, 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, Schedule C, and 1040
- **Business Documents** – Offer letters and invoices

## Features

**Accurate Calculations** – Federal and state taxes, deductions, and YTD totals computed per pay period

**PDF Rendering** – Documents rendered with mPDF and headless Chrome, with multi-document PDF merging

**Stripe Payments** – Pay-per-document checkout (from $8.99 per paystub) through Laravel Cashier, with coupon codes

**User Accounts** – Saved documents, order history, and passwordless magic-link login

**Admin Dashboard** – Orders, customers, coupons, marketing ads, blocked IPs, and customer feedback

**Marketing Integrations** – Google Ads, Facebook Pixel and Conversions API, Google Tag Manager, and an auto-generated sitemap

**Background Jobs** – Redis queues managed with Laravel Horizon, Mailgun email delivery, S3 file storage, and scheduled backups

## Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Laravel 13 (PHP 8.4), Laravel Cashier, Horizon, Sanctum
- **Frontend:** React + TypeScript via Inertia.js
- **Styling:** TailwindCSS + shadcn/ui (Radix UI), GSAP animations
- **PDF:** mPDF, headless Chrome, PDF merge
- **Infrastructure:** Redis, AWS S3, Mailgun, Laravel Nightwatch
- **Payments:** Stripe
