# PracticeScope

## Overview

PracticeScope is an AI-powered acquisition analysis tool for dentists buying a practice. Users upload a P&L, tax return, or practice summary, get 30+ benchmarked diagnostic metrics instantly, and can buy a personalized AI acquisition playbook for $7, so they never overpay for a practice.

## How It Works

**Upload or Enter Data** – Drop in a P&L, tax return, or practice summary. AI extracts and fills 50+ fields automatically.

**Instant Diagnostics** – 30+ metrics across production, overhead, collections, hygiene, and patient flow, each benchmarked, rated, and paired with a dollar-amount upside.

**AI Acquisition Report** – A $7 playbook with negotiation talking points, risk flags, growth strategies, and valuation modeling.

## Features

**30+ Diagnostic Metrics** – Benchmarked against industry standards, with the upside found in each area

**AI Document Parsing** – PDFs are read in the browser and AI extracts 50+ fields from P&Ls, tax returns, and practice summaries

**AI Acquisition Playbook** – Streaming AI report with negotiation points, risk assessment, and growth strategies

**Valuation & Loan Modeling** – Purchase price, loan scenarios, and projected cash flow

**Exportable PDF Reports** – Reports for lenders, partners, and advisors

**Stripe Payments** – Free to explore, pay only for AI reports, no subscription

**Analysis History & Admin Dashboard** – Saved analyses for users, and full visibility into users, analyses, and revenue for admins

## Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Go + PocketBase (API, auth, file storage, admin)
- **Frontend:** React 19 + TypeScript, React Router, TanStack Query
- **Styling:** TailwindCSS + shadcn/ui (Radix UI)
- **AI:** OpenAI and Anthropic APIs behind one provider interface, streamed with the Vercel AI SDK
- **PDF:** pdf.js for reading uploads, jsPDF for report export
- **Payments:** Stripe
