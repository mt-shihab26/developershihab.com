# Lucid Margins

## Overview

Lucid Margins is a financial analysis web app for small and mid-sized businesses, and for accountants and advisors who manage many client companies. Users connect QuickBooks Online or upload a spreadsheet, track 51 KPIs against their targets, and ask Margo, the built-in finance assistant, why a number moved.

## How It Works

**Connect** – Link QuickBooks Online through OAuth (synced nightly) or upload an Excel/CSV file. Budgets can be imported too.

**Track** – 51 built-in KPIs across profitability, working capital, liquidity and coverage, and returns and growth. Each KPI is marked on-track or off-track against its target.

**Ask** – Margo answers questions about the numbers. It is read-only, scoped to one company at a time, and only quotes figures pulled from the company's own data.

## Features

**51 KPIs + Custom KPIs** – Built-in metrics plus a formula builder for custom KPIs, with 19 alert types

**Nine Analysis Views** – Profitability, Cash Flow, Growth, Trend, Financials (P&L with EBITDA layout, balance sheet, cash flow statement, common-size view), KPI Numbers, KPI Explorer, Goal Seek, and Alerts

**Goal Seek** – Shows how much price, volume, cost of sales, fixed or variable expenses must change to hit a target such as net margin

**Margo AI Assistant** – A read-only finance chat assistant grounded in the company's data

**Live Reports** – Seven section types, eleven templates, saved custom templates, drafts and published versions, comments, present mode, PDF download, and Excel export

**Organizations** – Many client companies under one login, Owner/Admin/Member roles, email invites, and isolated data per company

**Security** – Two-factor authentication, passkeys, and strong password rules

## Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Laravel 13 (PHP 8.5), Laravel Fortify, Laravel AI, PhpSpreadsheet
- **Frontend:** React 19 + TypeScript via Inertia.js
- **Styling:** TailwindCSS + shadcn/ui
- **Integrations:** QuickBooks Online (OAuth), Excel/CSV import
- **Reports:** React PDF, Excel export
